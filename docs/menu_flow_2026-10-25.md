# Beta9 menu and setup review — 25 October 2026

The earlier illustrated home and kingdom picker did not carry through to Settings, pause, results and management sheets. The setup header also mixed Back, Solo/Online tabs and kingdom selection, while More options expanded inside a short sidebar. This pass makes those reachable surfaces part of the same forest-cloth, warm-paper and honey-action palette and separates navigation from the match draft.

## Decisions and preserved behavior

- Home chooses Solo or Online. Setup has a dedicated Back/title/Settings row; its former duplicate mode tabs are hidden. Back returns to the existing mode choice without discarding the draft.
- Kingdom sits immediately above the three battlefield cards. Selected cards retain a border and explicit selected word. On the shortest UI-scaled screens with browser toolbar/cutout clearance, map thumbnails give way to complete map names and selected state; all three targets remain visible.
- Difficulty and match rules stay beside the maps. Population, optional seed and opening tips have a dedicated Match options sheet with a fixed header and Done action. The current population maximum remains in the launch summary. All five population choices, both rulesets, three difficulties, automatic rival civilization and seed behavior are preserved.
- The options sheet contains keyboard focus, restores it on close, blocks covered launch/mode controls and keeps browser updates unsafe until closed. Escape closes the sheet before returning Home.
- Settings has a title and clear active tabs. Audio, Controls, Display and the expandable Guide use the same surfaces and type. Narrow preference rows stack their label above controls; switching tabs retains the supplied safe area. Reset still resets application preferences while preserving the draft match.
- Pause, the fixed-header Field guide, building sheets, worker/army management, selection Info, results and native report confirmation use the shared palette. Production paging appears only when multiple eligible buildings exist, with readable chevrons and accessible names. Recruitment, queues, rally orders, construction and timing are unchanged.

All art is existing project art. No third-party image or sound assets were introduced. The civilization comparison keeps its warm paper surface; the three towns/crests and their gameplay definitions are unchanged. The old unused AgeUpDialog scene is not an active flow and was not reactivated.
