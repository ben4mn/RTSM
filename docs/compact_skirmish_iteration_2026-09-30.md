## Design reference and scope

The official AoE II guide describes archers countering most infantry, spear units countering cavalry, cavalry countering archers, and an economy adjusted to the current military plan. Our Warrior is now explicitly spear infantry, with a spear silhouette; Archer uses a bow, and Horseman is fast cavalry. These are a small adaptation, not an AoE roster or imported balance table. [Official military/economy guide](https://www.ageofempires.com/learn-to-play/military-and-economy-aoe2/)

Workers, military, population capacity, paid production and resource delivery form one loop. Contextual commands and visible idle-worker information follow the official interface examples. AoE IV's quickstart also connects exploration, gathering, construction, armies and Sacred control. [Official resource/interface guide](https://www.ageofempires.com/learn-to-play/control-resources-aoe2/), [official AoE IV quickstart](https://www.ageofempires.com/news/quickstart-guide-age-of-empires-iv/)

The target remains compact 20/30/40-population skirmishes. Siege is omitted from these build/AI choices. No new campaign, faction, technology tree or free economy was added. The numbers below are local design decisions.

## Battlefield and command space

The map grows from 40×40 to 48×48 tiles: 1,600 → 2,304 tiles, **44% more tile area** and 20% more tiles per dimension. The central clearing is 15×15 tiles, with seven-tile-wide carved approaches. This expands traversable contest space; it does not mean 44% more battlefield is visible within the same phone screen.

Six-seed production checks verify reachable bases/resources, open center, and optional guidance ending. An independent 500-baseline/500-current-map sweep retained four nearby home berries and nearby home wood in every sampled home; the current central clearing had no forest/water/resource intrusions. The sweep measures presence/distance, while the smaller production checks exercise reachability. [Battlefield check](/Users/ben/Documents/1_Projects/aoem/output/rts-iteration-2026-09-30/battlefield-room.log), [independent audit](/Users/ben/Documents/1_Projects/aoem/output/aoe-mobile-rts/ai/critical-root-gameplay-audit.md)

## Touch, selection and working villagers

Canceled or orphan contacts no longer order units. HUD-origin contacts released in the world stay owned by the HUD; both pinch release orders recover. Camera drag requires a world-owned contact. Placement ignores synthesized mouse events: real ScreenTouch dispatch through House card → world preview spends nothing until explicit Place, while a native desktop mouse still confirms normally.

The ordinary 0.5-second selection refresh formerly hid the command row briefly, canceling a held Clear. Actual 0.55-second Clear now survives that refresh. Dense troop tests cover 40 units and 360 body taps; a near-exact ground marker wins over overlapping tall bodies.

Hands-on play also exposed tree canopies swallowing badges and routing taps to Move, resource-body priority swallowing hostile attacks, and Farm facade taps dropping the selected worker. Visible resource bodies now route to Gather under ownership/activity/stock/current-vision guards; exact hostile targets retain Attack priority. Farm outer-art touch and desktop right-click have separate checks. Ordinary newborns use balanced reachable assignments unless a deliberate rally applies. Paid construction preserves cargo and existing jobs, rejects stale targets, and recovers from destroyed jobs.

**Latest builder-loop correction:** a naturally loaded worker approaching its drop-off was in MOVING. Construction completed and its cargo still deposited, but the old resume logic chose a nearer same-type resource instead of returning to the original job. The fix preserves the target/knowledge/cargo only for an active drop-off work loop. Empty resource approaches already resumed their exact target; manual Move and flee must not resume stale work. Natural paid before/after checks verify the original tree, ten-wood deposit, and bank change of −50 wood for House +10 deposit. This was not evidence that all walking workers abandoned foundations. Final-source paid probes and both paid three-role hands-on replays are complete, with the observation limits below. [Before reproduction](/Users/ben/Documents/1_Projects/aoem/output/aoe-mobile-rts/ai/active-dropoff-build-before-fix.log), [after check](/Users/ben/Documents/1_Projects/aoem/output/aoe-mobile-rts/ai/active-dropoff-build-after-fix.log), [input/economy audit](/Users/ben/Documents/1_Projects/aoem/output/aoe-mobile-rts/ai/critical-root-gameplay-audit.md)

**Confirmed human worker-recovery limitation:** damage triggers auto-flee only while a worker is GATHERING or IDLE; MOVING and BUILDING workers do not auto-flee. Fleeing issues the same manual Move path used for explicit movement, cancels the pending gather order and drop-off callback, preserves carried cargo, and arrives IDLE without automatically returning to work. The human must assign gathering again; the Idle button selects/cycles workers rather than assigning a job. Farm completion resumes a valid saved previous resource job, not automatic gathering from the new Farm; an empty idle builder or manually canceled builder can remain Idle. [Damage-state guard](/Users/ben/Documents/1_Projects/aoem/scripts/units/villager.gd:80), [flee path](/Users/ben/Documents/1_Projects/aoem/scripts/units/villager.gd:511), [pending-order cancellation](/Users/ben/Documents/1_Projects/aoem/scripts/units/villager.gd:794), [arrival to Idle](/Users/ben/Documents/1_Projects/aoem/scripts/units/unit_base.gd:303), [saved construction job](/Users/ben/Documents/1_Projects/aoem/scripts/units/villager.gd:448).

AI separately reassigns Idle workers. The player-0 Medium policy bot in the paid probes therefore does **not establish human post-raid recovery usability**, even when its paid economy recovers. Source inspection establishes the behavior above but cannot attribute each observed Idle worker to fleeing rather than canceled movement, finished construction or another cause. This remains a critical playability limitation in the frozen runtime.

**Cargo can misleadingly appear as active gathering.** The selected Villager action reads “Gathering” whenever `carried_amount > 0`, even when its actual state is IDLE or MOVING. Task totals also classify MOVING workers with a carried resource type as gathering without checking an active work/drop-off callback. A loaded fleeing or manually moved worker can therefore display active-looking resource work after its job was canceled. Readable text does not establish accurate employment feedback; this remains unfixed alongside the need to reassign Idle workers. [Selected action formatting](/Users/ben/Documents/1_Projects/aoem/scripts/main/main.gd:1638), [worker task classification](/Users/ben/Documents/1_Projects/aoem/scripts/main/main.gd:1158).

**Town Center provides no attack or garrison defense.** Its data contain production, drop-off, population and vision, but no attack damage/range; BuildingBase defaults those fields to zero and only runs auto-attack when damage is positive. The runtime has no garrison command or occupancy system. A retreat to the Town Center therefore does not itself provide protective fire or shelter, despite the flee code preferring that destination. This establishes source behavior, not the reason for every observed worker loss. [Town Center data](/Users/ben/Documents/1_Projects/aoem/scripts/data/building_data.gd:25), [attack defaults](/Users/ben/Documents/1_Projects/aoem/scripts/buildings/building_base.gd:117), [attack gate](/Users/ben/Documents/1_Projects/aoem/scripts/buildings/building_base.gd:300), [Town Center scene](/Users/ben/Documents/1_Projects/aoem/scenes/buildings/town_center.tscn).

## AI economy, difficulty and paid diagnostics

Safe resource deliveries survive evacuation; exposed workers retreat instead of repeatedly canceling every delivery. Scarce-food recovery preserves worker food and restores food labor. Compact production banks for both Feudal producers, ranks living plus queued roles before affordability, excludes Siege, and uses current paid costs. Scouts do not count as fighting strength. Counter preferences use visible enemies; search destinations derive from public map symmetry and remembered targets are positions.

Urgent Sacred contest uses 75% of living fighters and all in the last 120 seconds, subject to visible home threats. Easy ordinary offense waits until 300 seconds; home defense and urgent Sacred response can override that opening gate.

Independent runtime calls found old compact Easy and Medium both targeting 15 mature workers plus 14 fighters at 30 population; Easy's smaller home reserve could actually leave a larger field wave. This code finding is independent of operator losses. The final Easy learning profile uses **12 workers, at most nine living-plus-queued fighters and one paid Scout** at 30 population; 20/40 use 8/16 workers and 6/12 fighters. Equal starting banks, shared costs/stats and no resource gifts remain enforced.
