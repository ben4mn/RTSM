# Seeded battlefields and trade: balance evidence (2026-10-26)

The candidate starts with **three villagers**, each gathering 1.8 resources per tick (previously two villagers at 2.0). Starting and recruited villagers, including AI workers, use the same per-civilization rate and shared researched upgrades. Food and wood still tick every 0.75 seconds; gold every 0.9 seconds. This keeps three simultaneous opening assignments without giving the opening workforce a hidden bonus.

## Calculated economy, not match-duration observations

With capacity 30, speed 60 and a 120-world-unit one-way haul, delivered income is `30 * 60 / (30 / raw_rate + 2 * 120 / 60)`. Food/wood deliver **109.09 per worker/minute** and gold **94.74**, versus 118.03 and 102.86 before: reductions of 7.6% and 7.9% after travel. Three identical food/wood workers have theoretical capacity 327.27/minute; four have 436.36. Actual mixed assignments, building, attacks, farm setup and travel lower those figures.

Across the 60 tested seed/style combinations, whole-map natural stock was 7,917-7,998 food, 15,014-16,000 wood and 5,966-6,000 gold, with exactly mirrored per-tile stock. Dense woodland no longer multiplies the finite economy. Starting timber retains 75 per tree and expansion timber 55; wilderness shares the remaining stock. For each player's approximate half, five lumberjacks need about 14.7 productive minutes to deliver 8,000 wood, five food gatherers need 7.3 productive minutes for 4,000 food, and two gold miners need 15.8 productive minutes for 3,000 gold. Ramp-up and travel add time; unvisited deposits do not disappear automatically. These estimates motivate farms and trade in prolonged games but **do not prove all finite resources exhaust at 20 minutes**.

A Trade Post costs 200 wood in Feudal, has 850 HP, and produces a 60 wood/40 gold Trader in 22 seconds for two population. The post must be at least 500 world units from an owned Town Center. A trader first visits its TC, then its post, then returns to the TC before receiving gold. Each completed round trip pays floor(distance * 0.04), from 20 to 60 gold, with three seconds handling per endpoint. At 600 and 1,200 world-unit straight routes, modeled income is about 62 and 72 gold/minute respectively before collision/path detours; longer blocked routes earn less. The 60-gold cap prevents unlimited long-route scaling. Destroyed endpoints, killed traders, Stop and manual retasking interrupt income and discard undelivered cargo. There is no passive building gold.

## Engine tests

`test_generated_battlefields.gd`: 60 cases across all five styles and twelve seeds, each generated twice. Exact grid equality, reflected terrain and resource stock, reachable starting locations/objective/expansions/every resource, and open village cores passed. Seeded crossings, lane widths, home-resource orientation and expansion locations vary. Twin Lakes and Golden Basin are distinct styles. This validates generated terrain; construction blockades remain gameplay and are handled by navigation.

`test_trade_routes.tscn`: an actual unit traversed a generated map and delivered exactly 39 gold after 41.35 simulated seconds. Stop/resume, endpoint destruction, ownership rejection and exact owner-bank credit passed. This is a controlled engine fixture, not a human match. `test_economy_balance.gd` verifies the reduced rates, fractional +25% upgrade, finite depletion, capacity and real deposits. `test_farm_economy.gd` verifies ownership, permanence, input and harvest/drop-off.

## Bounded paid comparisons

Two standard 100-population Medium-versus-Medium simulations use seed 424242, both civilizations pinned to French/Crownlands, normal paid production, and fixed 60 FPS simulation input. The only comparison variable is three versus four starting villagers. At 240 game seconds, player 0 delivered 1,020F/784W/157G with three workers versus 1,361F/1,065W/187G with four. First damage contact occurred at 183.35 versus 182.92 seconds. Four produced 33.2% more total deposits at this checkpoint without materially changing this AI's initial attack timing. Three is the conservative candidate for avoiding an inflated opening bank and preserving investment choices.

Full simulation results will be appended when these bounded runs finish. No population-average match-duration or win-rate claim follows from a single mirrored matchup. The desired 6-9 minute fast wins, 12-15 minute average and 20+ minute prolonged games remain pacing targets pending a broader human sample.
