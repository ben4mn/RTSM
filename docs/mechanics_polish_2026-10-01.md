## Resulting behavior

- Economic workers can notice a visible armed enemy approaching before the first hit, retreat into a legal refuge inside friendly defensive range, retain cargo and the accepted gather/build job, and resume when safe. Manual orders remain authoritative. Hidden enemies, harmless Scouts, stationary distant enemies, and manual Move/Stop/Attack do not trigger this response. Safe alternative resources are searched within a bounded budget; complete resource denial can still stall work.
- Work and cargo are reported separately. Returning a partial old load does not invent an ongoing gathering job. Missing depots display waiting feedback; a paid replacement depot permits exactly one deposit. Completed Farms accept their idle builders, while abandoned owned foundations say **Needs builder**. Genuine travel or recovery for a still-owned Build order continues to show construction progress.
- Completed Town Centers fire one visible arrow for 6 damage every 2 seconds within 8 tiles / 128 world units, with armor resolved at release and quick idle reacquisition. Their selected range ring matches the actual firing disk. Units receive priority; foundations, hidden enemies, and buildings are not valid arrow targets. Towers retain their previous stats. This helps against small raids; a large army can still overwhelm the TC.
- AI recovery counts living workers and real paid completions, preserves worker retreat, reserves worker Food during severe loss, distributes idle workers against actual labor gaps, and delays optional expenses while rebuilding. It banks Wood to unlock military roles and adjusts committed army shares and visible target assignments using counter matchups. Landed-hit telemetry records resolved attacks instead of inferring Horseman combat from casualties.

Runtime changes are in `scripts/units/villager.gd`, `scripts/units/unit_base.gd`, `scripts/units/combat.gd`, `scripts/buildings/building_base.gd`, `scripts/data/building_data.gd`, `scripts/ai/ai_controller.gd`, `scripts/main/main.gd`, and `scripts/ui/hud.gd`. Gameplay data remains typed and map routing uses the existing canonical conversions.
