## Scope and references

The owner asked for fewer permanent words/buttons and more recognizable icons with expanding menus, citing mobile League of Legends. This pass follows the larger-map release; it changes presentation and menu geometry, not maps, gathering, AI, prices, timers or victory rules. ## Player-facing changes

- Original outlined icons replace permanent Base/Economy/Army, View, area-selection, Move/Attack, More, Clear, Pause and Build captions. Army, queue and idle counts remain badges; economy attention remains visible. Selected/armed actions stay highlighted.
- Existing exclusive menus disclose secondary choices. Expanded management sheets leave their launchers exposed; tapping the same launcher closes the sheet. More retains short action names. Info and optional holds retain full explanations.
- Recruitment restores unit portraits, keeps names and every numerical resource cost, and moves queue counts into badges. Repeat keeps its unit, ON/OFF state and resource/population wait reason.
- Build cards retain names, prices and requirements while showing more choices across the phone. Finite Farm stock remains explicit. Resource counters use distinct symbols and compact values, with exact totals in accessible labels and tooltips.
- Primary targets retain at least 48 logical pixels. Decorative icons/badges ignore pointer input, and recurring refreshes preserve existing button identity and input ownership.
