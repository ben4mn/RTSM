## What players can do

- View, More, Queue, Info, Help and the world popup share one disclosure lifecycle. View stays open for repeated zoom adjustments, then closes after Center/Reset. Secondary menus close when battlefield interaction starts or selection actually changes. Routine HP, cargo and production refreshes preserve the menu and its live controls.
- Hold Train, Repeat, research, refunds or a Build card to preview costs and requirements without spending, canceling a queue, toggling Repeat or entering placement. Disabled action buttons can still explain requirements. A progress cue precedes the card; release closes it. The bounded preview pins a reminder to use tap Info for all details, and shows no inert scrollbar.
- Hold More, slide to a labeled action, then release. Stop, Patrol, Stance and Idle workers use existing handlers. Returning to the starting area or releasing outside cancels. Patrol arms its existing destination mode and still requires a world destination.
- Tap the selection summary's Info handle to read the same producer explanations in a scrollable card. Build has an Info toggle: tap any card, including locked or unaffordable cards, to inspect it. Close returns to the same Build category. Leaving inspection restores purchase restrictions.
- Settings can disable the new hold shortcuts. Ordinary taps and tap inspection remain available. Existing world hold behavior stays available at its original 350 ms threshold; GUI holds use a 450 ms prototype threshold. Both thresholds use real time at every simulation speed.
- Drag directly on a Build or production card to scroll its row. A canceled drag cannot later become a purchase. Outside dismissal owns every contact through release, including added fingers. Escape closes the current secondary surface before clearing selection. Pause remains reachable in one tap and takes modal priority.

No inactivity timer closes a menu while reading prices or waiting for resources. Information and chooser geometry remain fixed for their owning contact. Resize, lost focus, selection replacement, another finger or modal interruption cancels the pending action. Emulated mouse events are reconciled with their originating touch so they cannot create a second purchase.

Queue freezes its page choice when pressed, so a delayed scroll restoration cannot reverse the action before release. Actual selection or modal changes cancel the pending press; a cosmetic refresh of the same producer preserves it. The regression admits real input, injects the old restore during the press and verifies fully reachable Undo/Clear controls, unchanged resources and subsequent paid refunds.
